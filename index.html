Micro-Scale Non-Equilibrium Gas Dynamics & Homogeneous Condensation

Governing equations and thermodynamic phase transformations during supersonic expansion of cold-gas propellants in CubeSat MEMS micro-nozzles.

1. Micro-Machined Convergent-Divergent Expansion

Thermodynamics

In cold-gas micro-propulsion (pure $N_2$ or $R-236fa$), stagnant propellant expands isentropically across a micro-throat of hydraulic diameter $D_h \approx 45\,\mu\text{m}$. As the gas trades thermal enthalpy for bulk velocity, temperature plummets according to the Mach area relation:

$$\frac{T(x)}{T_0} = \left(1 + \frac{\gamma - 1}{2} M(x)^2 \right)^{-1}, \quad v(x) = M(x) \sqrt{\gamma R T(x)}$$

Where $M(x)$ is local Mach number, $\gamma = 1.4$ for diatomic nitrogen, and $R = 296.8\,\text{J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$. At exit area ratio $A_e/A^* \approx 15$, $M_e > 3.4$, dropping gas temperature below the nitrogen sublimation line ($T_{exit} < 63\,\text{K}$).

Boundary Layer Correction: Thick Knudsen boundary layers reduce effective $A/A^*$ by $18\%$.

2. Cluster Freeze-Out & Nucleation Kinetics

Phase Transition

When vapor supersaturation $S = P(x) / P_{\text{sat}}(T) \gg 1$, molecular nitrogen molecules collide and stick, overcoming the free energy activation barrier $\Delta G^*$ to form solid sub-nanometer clusters. The homogeneous nucleation rate $J$ is modeled via Frenkel-Zeldovich kinetics:

$$J = \left( \frac{2\sigma}{\pi m} \right)^{1/2} \frac{\rho_v^2}{\rho_l} \exp \left( -\frac{16\pi \sigma^3 m^2}{3(k_B T)^3 (\rho_l \ln S)^2} \right)$$

Solid aerosol clusters rob latent heat from the flow while simultaneously dragging gas molecules, producing condensation shock discontinuities and scattering plume momentum vectors away from the thrust axis.

Droplet Size: Critical nucleus radius $r^* = 2\sigma m / (\rho_l k_B T \ln S) \approx 0.82\,\text{nm}$.

3. Specific Impulse & Stationkeeping Penalty

Orbital Mechanics

When a condensate fraction $\chi = \dot{m}_{\text{cond}} / \dot{m}_{\text{total}}$ forms in the divergent section, the two-phase flow experiences momentum slippage and irreversible entropy generation. The degraded specific impulse $I_{sp}$ collapses:

$$I_{sp,\text{eff}} = \frac{(1 - \chi) v_g + \chi v_p + (P_e - P_\infty)A_e / \dot{m}}{g_0} \approx I_{sp,\text{nom}} \left( 1 - \beta \chi - \Delta \eta_{\text{thermal}} \right)$$
